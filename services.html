Service Offerings

Each offering is a fixed-scope engagement with defined deliverables, target buyer, and evidence package. Designed for procurement under existing federal vehicles, subcontract teaming, or direct award.

Service 01

Patch Compliance Assessment + Remediation Plan

Identify compliance gaps, isolate top failure drivers, and produce an actionable remediation plan.

Request Scope Discussion
Best for
Organizations with patch compliance below internal targets or audit thresholds, particularly those running MECM/SCCM or Intune at enterprise scale.
Deliverables may include
  • Patch compliance baseline across managed estate
  • Environment health review and dependency map
  • Root-cause summary of top compliance failure drivers
  • Prioritized remediation backlog with effort estimates
  • Up to three quick-win recommendations or fixes where appropriate
  • Executive summary for leadership review
  • Updated runbook guidance for sustained operations
Service 02

Intune / Windows Autopatch Readiness + Pilot Plan

Prepare Microsoft endpoint environments for a controlled, governed rollout.

Request Scope Discussion
Best for
Organizations adopting Intune or Windows Autopatch and needing a sequenced rollout plan with deployment rings, policy baselines, and validation criteria.
Deliverables may include
  • Readiness assessment of current endpoint state
  • Deployment ring strategy and population mapping
  • Policy baseline approach (compliance and configuration)
  • Enrollment approach across device classes
  • Pilot plan with target population and success criteria
  • Validation and rollback approach
Service 03

MDM Migration Blueprint

Plan a phased migration from legacy MDM/UEM platforms to modern endpoint management.

Request Scope Discussion
Best for
Organizations on AirWatch / Workspace ONE planning consolidation to Intune or another UEM, particularly with mixed iOS and Windows fleets.
Deliverables may include
  • Current-state inventory of devices, policies, profiles, and apps
  • Policy / profile / application mapping strategy to target platform
  • Pilot design with representative populations
  • Phased cutover plan and dependency sequencing
  • Risk register and mitigation approach
  • User communication templates and rollout messaging
Service 04

CMMC Level 1 / FAR 52.204-21 Readiness Sprint

Help contractors organize basic cyber hygiene evidence and remediation priorities.

Request Scope Discussion
Best for
DoD subcontractors and suppliers with FAR 52.204-21 flowdown obligations preparing for CMMC Level 1 assertion or assessment.
Deliverables may include
  • Control checklist mapped to current operational state
  • Endpoint and access-control evidence map
  • Device inventory review and gap analysis
  • Patch and vulnerability backlog summary
  • MFA and account-control documentation review
  • SOP / policy gap list
  • Executive remediation roadmap with sequencing
Compliance positioning

Timechain provides readiness, implementation, remediation, documentation, and operational support. Timechain does not provide official CMMC certification or act as a C3PAO unless separately qualified and engaged.

Service 05

NIST 800-171 / CUI Readiness Roadmap

Develop a phased roadmap toward NIST 800-171 alignment for organizations handling Controlled Unclassified Information.

Request Scope Discussion
Best for
Defense contractors and subcontractors processing CUI who need a sequenced plan toward NIST 800-171 alignment ahead of CMMC Level 2 obligations.

  • System Security Plan (SSP) skeleton or update support
  • Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M) framework
  • Endpoint and identity control documentation review

  • Evidence collection approach and templates
