01 / Company Overview
Who We Are

Timechain Technologies LLC is a veteran-owned small business focused on practical IT modernization, endpoint operations, cyber readiness, and AI-enabled workflow improvement for federal, defense, and regulated organizations.

The company is positioned for low-friction subcontract support, fixed-scope assessments, readiness sprints, and rapid technical execution. Founded by a U.S. Air Force veteran with 15+ years of enterprise IT operations experience supporting ~20,000 endpoints across Windows and iOS environments.
