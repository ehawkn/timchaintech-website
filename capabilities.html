Capabilities

Timechain Technologies LLC supports federal, defense, and regulated organizations with practical IT modernization services designed to improve security posture, operational visibility, endpoint compliance, and delivery speed.

01 / Endpoint

Endpoint Modernization

Move from fragmented legacy management to a unified, governed endpoint platform — Microsoft Intune, Endpoint Manager, Windows Autopatch, and MECM/SCCM modernization with lifecycle controls.

  • Microsoft Intune / Endpoint Manager design and operations
  • Windows Autopatch strategy, readiness, and rollout governance
  • MECM/SCCM modernization and migration planning
  • Application deployment pipelines
  • Compliance policies and configuration profiles
  • Endpoint lifecycle governance and retirement workflows
  • Deployment ring strategy and validation criteria
  • Operational runbooks and support handoff packages
02 / Patch

Patch Compliance & Remediation

Move patch compliance numbers in the right direction with measurable workflow improvements, executive reporting, and remediation discipline.

  • Compliance baseline establishment
  • Failure-driver analysis and root-cause summary
  • Prioritized remediation backlog
  • Operational reporting and executive dashboards
  • Deployment governance and maintenance windows
  • Exception handling and policy documentation
  • Audit-ready operational evidence packages
  • Runbook development for sustained operations
03 / Mobility

Enterprise Mobility / MDM / UEM

Migrate from AirWatch / Workspace ONE to modern UEM platforms with phased cutovers, rollback planning, and stakeholder communications built in.

  • AirWatch / Workspace ONE to Intune / UEM migration
  • iOS device governance and compliance
  • Mobile compliance and conditional access
  • Policy, profile, and application mapping
  • Pilot design and validation
  • Phased cutovers and rollback planning
  • User communication templates and rollout planning
  • Post-cutover support and operational handoff
04 / Cyber

Cyber Readiness

Help contractors and subcontractors prepare for CMMC and NIST-aligned environments with endpoint evidence, controls documentation, and remediation planning.

  • CMMC Level 1 readiness support
  • FAR 52.204-21 basic safeguarding alignment
  • NIST 800-171 roadmap support
  • Endpoint evidence packages
  • Device inventory and asset management review
  • MFA and account-control documentation
  • Policies, SOPs, and operational runbooks
  • Supplier flowdown and prime contractor support
Compliance positioning

Timechain provides readiness, implementation, remediation, documentation, and operational support. Timechain does not provide official CMMC certification or act as a C3PAO unless separately qualified and engaged.

05 / AI Ops

AI-Enabled Operations

Apply AI to real operational workflows — documentation, retrieval, remediation playbooks, and service desk acceleration — within governance and security constraints.

  • Private IT Ops knowledge assistant readiness
  • AI-assisted SOP and runbook generation
  • Remediation playbook development
  • Service desk workflow automation
  • Incident summary and post-mortem support
  • Documentation structuring and tagging
  • Governance-aware AI adoption planning
  • Sensitive-data handling considerations
06 / Teaming

Subcontract & Teaming Support

Senior technical capacity for prime contractors, with fixed-scope work packages, task-order support, and surge capacity built for federal delivery cadences.

  • Prime contractor technical support
  • Targeted staff augmentation
  • Fixed-scope work packages
  • Task-order delivery support
  • Surge capacity for compressed timelines
